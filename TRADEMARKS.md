## Use freely, no permission needed

- Say that your software works with, runs on, or is built from Bivy, as long as
  it's accurate and doesn't suggest we endorse you.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and books about Bivy.
- Run Bivy for yourself, your team or your company, and call it Bivy
  internally.
- Distribute unmodified Bivy releases under the name Bivy.

## Ask first

- Offering Bivy, or a modified version, as a hosted service to people outside
  your organization under a name that contains "Bivy" (for example "Bivy
  Hosting" or "Acme Bivy Cloud").
- Distributing a modified version under the name Bivy. Use a different name and
  say it is "based on Bivy" instead.
- Using the logo, or anything confusingly similar, in your product, domain name
  or company name.
- Merchandise.

## Never

- Suggesting that your product or service is Bivy Cloud or is operated,
  sponsored or endorsed by the Bivy project when it isn't.

You're welcome to fork Bivy under the AGPL. If you distribute or host a fork,
give it its own name and logo. You can describe it as "a fork of Bivy".
