Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026

Bivy is a local-first, open-source AI agent workspace that runs on your own machine. For launch, Bivy Cloud is the hosted control plane and relay; it is not a hosted AI inference service. This policy explains what the hosted services do and do not collect. It does not apply to software you run entirely on your own hardware without signing in to the hosted service.

The short version. Your agent sessions — prompts, files, tool output, model API keys, provider OAuth tokens, and GitHub repo tokens — stay on your machine or in a vault you control. When you connect a phone through our relay, traffic is end-to-end encrypted and the relay only ever sees ciphertext. We never receive your session content or plaintext model credentials.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo create your account and send passwordless magic-link sign-in emails.
Billing data (via Stripe)If you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment. We store your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, plan, and subscription status — not your card details.
Node registry metadataThe identifier and display name you give each node, plus online/offline status and last-seen time, so you can manage your nodes.
Session/work metadataOptional cross-node session and webhook/work-queue metadata such as node id, session id, status, repo slug, issue number, and timestamps. This is routing metadata, not transcript content.
Encrypted credential sync blobsIf enabled, nodes may upload encrypted model-auth vault ciphertext and per-node wrapped keys so another enrolled node can sync credentials. Bivy Cloud cannot decrypt these blobs.
Operational logsConnection counts, timestamps, and error events used to keep the service running and detect abuse. These never contain session payloads.

What we never collect

  • The content of your agent sessions — prompts, responses, files, or tool output.
  • Your plaintext model provider API keys, provider OAuth tokens, GitHub repo tokens, or other local secrets.
  • Agent transcripts, terminal output, prompts you type into an active session, workspace files, or tool output.
  • Your model calls: Bivy Cloud does not proxy normal AI inference at launch.
  • The end-to-end encryption keys used between your node and your devices (the relay never holds them).

Third parties we share data with

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management (privacy policy).
  • Resend — delivery of sign-in and account emails (privacy policy).
  • Our hosting provider — runs the control plane and relay servers.

We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies and local storage

The web and remote-control interfaces store a session token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Data retention

We keep account and node metadata for as long as your account is active. Magic-link and connection tokens are short-lived and expire automatically. When you delete your account, we remove your account and node metadata; billing records may be retained where required by law.

Data controller

The data controller for the hosted services is Sjulstad Labs (Norwegian organization number 923640940), Norway. For any privacy or data-protection question, contact us at support@bivy.sh.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, contact the data controller, Sjulstad Labs, at support@bivy.sh.

Important note about work queues

If you enable the GitHub issue work-queue integration, the hosted control plane stores the incoming issue title and body long enough for your node to claim and run the work. Agent output and workspace data still stay on your node. We are evaluating end-to-end encryption for queued work-item content before broader paid launch.

Changes
